Summary

Data scientist and Registered Professional Biologist with six years of experience building reproducible data pipelines and statistical analyses for chemical, biological, and environmental data in regulated consulting work, and a Master of Data Science from UBC. Works in R, Python, and SQL with Git feature-branch workflows, unit testing and CI, and Docker/renv environments. At WSP, introduced Git-based development and R pipelines to the team. MSc research in analytical toxicology used high-resolution mass spectrometry and cell-based assays, with two peer-reviewed publications. Comfortable working across Unix/command-line tooling (Bash, Make) and translating between life sciences, engineering, and software teams.
